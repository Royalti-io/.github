# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Disagree about the work, not the person.

## What we expect

- Give feedback on code, ideas and decisions — directly, and without making it personal.
- Accept feedback the same way.
- Respect that people here work across very different time zones, first languages, and levels
  of familiarity with the music industry. "Obvious" usually isn't.
- Keep discussion on the repos to the work.

## What we won't accept

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, sexualised content, publishing
someone's private information, or sustained disruption of discussion.

## Reporting

Report anything that needs attention to `hello@royalti.io`. Reports are handled privately.
We'll respond as quickly as we reasonably can, and we won't discuss a report with anyone who
doesn't need to be involved.

Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, or block accounts that breach this document.
